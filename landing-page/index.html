THE PROBLEM

A drain blocks quietly. The flood arrives all at once.

In Colombo's low-lying neighbourhoods, a clogged drain gives no warning. In monsoon rain the water can go from ankle to waist in minutes, and by then homes and shops are already flooding. Councils have no live view of which drains are filling up or blocked.

For a family on the ground floor, those few minutes decide whether they get the fridge onto bricks, move the car to higher ground and get the kids upstairs, or just watch it happen.

WHY WE STARTED

This started with a flooded street, not a spreadsheet.

Every monsoon, the roads near our campus flood after one heavy afternoon of rain. The drains had been blocked for weeks, and nobody noticed until the water was in the street.

We're students, not a big company. We just think nobody should find out about a flood when it's already coming in under the door.

— Team Flood Force

HOW IT WORKS

Two thresholds, read straight from the drain.

Sense

A sealed sensor in the drain keeps reading the water level and checks it against a warning level and a threat level.

Warn

At the warning level, residents and the council get a notification. At the threat level, a siren goes off and an evacuation alert goes out.

Act

The dashboard shows which drains are rising or draining slowly, so crews can clear the real problem first.

WHAT IT DOES

Built for the street.

Two-stage warnings

A notification when the water starts rising, then a louder evacuation alarm if it hits the danger level.

Blocked-drain detection

If a drain empties slowly after the rain, we flag it as a likely blockage before it floods.

Live risk map

Every sensor knows where it is, so councils can see the high-risk spots and send crews where they're needed.

Community reports

Residents can report blocked drains and earn points when they're right, which helps crews get there faster.

FOR COUNCILS

One screen for every drain in the ward.

The dashboard puts every drain in the area on one map. Green is clear, amber is rising and red has crossed the threat level, so the duty officer can see the whole ward at a glance.
